Paycheck deductions

Money taken out of your paycheck before it reaches your bank (like a 401k). For each paycheck, this adds the amount as income, plus an expense in the category you pick (Savings for a 401k, Bills & Utilities for medical insurance), so your totals match what you really earned, saved and spent.

Categories & rules

Categories

Need and Want count toward Spent. Savings is money you set aside. Income is what the percentages are measured against. Ignore (transfers, card payments) counts toward nothing.

Rules

If a description contains the text (any case), it gets that category. Rules are checked top to bottom; first match wins. Categories you set by hand are never overwritten.
